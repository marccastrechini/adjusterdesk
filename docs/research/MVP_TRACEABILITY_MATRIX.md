## MVP Non-goals Confirmed

The study did not expect or evaluate legal advice, claim valuation, coverage determination, carrier integrations, QuickBooks sync, email/SMS automation beyond existing auth email flows, or advanced role permissions beyond current owner/adjuster/admin separation.
