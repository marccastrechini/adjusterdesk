# Automated Acquisition V1

## Goal
Low-engagement automated acquisition for AdjusterDesk under $200/month, focused on qualified public adjuster traffic with minimal manual outreach.

## Primary Channel
Google Search Ads micro-test.

## Budget
- $5/day for up to 30 days
- Hard monthly cap: $200
- Do not exceed $200/month without explicit approval

## Primary Landing Pages
- /public-adjuster-software
- /free-public-adjuster-claim-tracker
- /pricing

## Keywords
- "public adjuster software"
- "public adjuster crm"
- "public adjuster claim tracker"
- "public adjuster spreadsheet"
- "claim management software for public adjusters"
- "ClaimWizard alternative"

## Negative Keywords
- job
- salary
- license
- exam
- course
- how to become
- near me
- hire
- homeowner
- insurance company
- independent adjuster
- staff adjuster
- attorney

## Starting Campaign Structure
- Campaign: AdjusterDesk - Search - Public Adjusters
- Ad Group 1: Public Adjuster Software
- Ad Group 2: Claim Tracker / Spreadsheet
- Ad Group 3: Competitor Alternative (only if a competitor alternative landing page exists)

## Conversion Actions To Watch
- trial_start_click
- claim_tracker_download_click
- pricing_click
- signup

## Stop Rules
- Pause campaign if traffic is mostly homeowners, job seekers, license seekers, or unrelated insurance adjusters.
- Pause any keyword with spend but no relevant engagement after a reasonable test window.
- Do not exceed $200/month without explicit approval.
